Shark Tooth Island sits on a working tidal river. Paddling, boating, and fossil hunting carry real risk. This site is educational; it is not a guide service and does not guarantee conditions. You are responsible for your own safety: check weather and tides, wear a PFD, and do not attempt without the skills and gear for moving water.
